# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, whatever their age, body, size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps this project:

- Being patient with people who are learning, and generous with what you know.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use Opal, not for you.
- Showing empathy towards other people.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Responsibilities

Everyone taking part in this project is responsible for keeping the
environment welcoming and safe. Project maintainers are responsible for
clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour and are expected to take
fair, appropriate action in response to any instance of unacceptable
behaviour.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project uses — the issue tracker, the pull
requests, the discussions — and when someone is representing the project in
public.

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers. All
complaints will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly. Maintainers are
obliged to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with
this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor
for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
